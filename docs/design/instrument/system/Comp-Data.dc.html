An empty chart keeps its axes and says why it is empty. It never shows a zero line as if zero were data.
Chart rules. Series take chart-1 to chart-4 in order. A value is a solid bar; its container (in bed, a budget, capacity) is the same colour outlined. Mean and trend lines are a dashed 1.5 text line. Gridlines use line, the baseline control, and labels are text-2 mono. Tooltips are inverse. Every chart has a role="img" summary and a Show Table toggle with the same numbers.
